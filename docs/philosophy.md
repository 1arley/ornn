# Philosophy

Ornn should not be the agent that does the work. Ornn should be the knowledge that
makes the agent better at doing the work.

Skills teach mental models, questions, investigation, evidence and false-positive
boundaries. References centralize where to look. Patterns transfer solutions between
products. Recipes and collections make complexity discoverable without imposing an
execution engine. Deterministic detectors complement contextual reasoning.

Research is proportional to uncertainty, impact and irreversibility. Evidence outranks
speculation. External work is adapted, never copied blindly. Project-specific facts
stay with the project.

Complexity belongs inside the library; simplicity belongs at the interface.
